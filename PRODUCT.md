# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, Vanilla CSS, TypeScript, Vanilla Three.js

## Users

Recruiters, Hiring Managers, CTOs, Founders, and Agencies evaluating Md. Fazley Rabbi for backend and full-stack engineering roles.

## Product Purpose

An interactive personal hiring microsite designed to convert interested recruiters into conversations or interviews by demonstrating real production engineering experience, technical judgment, and AI-assisted development skills.

## Positioning

"I build systems that survive production."
The site acts as a living production system representing an engineer's experience, providing proof over simple claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors will explore this site as a scrollable website with an interactive 3D WebGL background (the "Engineering Core"). They expect fast loading times, no logins, and immediate access to CV/contact information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Must work without JavaScript/WebGL (static fallback).
- No backend required for MVP (no Supabase, MySQL, Auth).
- Deterministic chat interface for the "Ask Fazley" section (no real LLM).
- Highly performant (lazy-loaded 3D, paused when off-screen, reduced motion support).
- Must include data extracted from local Tinkering Lab (`/Users/rabbi/Desktop/Projects/Sites/astro-portfolio`) and Homelab (`/Volumes/1TB SSD/VPS-Backup`).

## Brand Commitments

- Tone: Confident, technical, direct, human, self-aware, slightly sarcastic, and occasionally funny.
- Avoid generic terms like "Rockstar", "Ninja", or "Guru".
- Visual Metaphor: A living software system, dark abstract environment, glowing connections.

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified 5+ years of PHP/Laravel experience.
- Experience with Payments (PayPal, Checkout.com, Stripe, Apple Pay).
- High-volume e-commerce and fraud detection systems.
- ClickHouse analytics and Redis caching.
- Content to be sourced from actual local projects (astro-portfolio, VPS-Backup).

## Product Principles

1. **Proof Over Claims:** Show actual architectures, decisions, and problem-solving over generic skill lists.
2. **Do Not Block Content:** Interactive experiences (3D) must never hide essential information or slow down the recruiter's understanding.
3. **Engineering Rigor:** The site itself must be a demonstration of good engineering (performance, lazy-loading, clean architecture).
4. **Distinctive Personality:** Blend professional production work with a curious "Tinkering Lab" and "Homelab" operator persona.
